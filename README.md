**Solis City: Coral Coast** is an open-world 3D WebGL action adventure built in Three.js, inspired by the sun-drenched neon metropolis of next-generation crime sagas. Navigate a sprawling urban paradise, command high-performance supercars, engage tactical law enforcement, and rise through syndicate ranks.

---

## Key Features

1. **Procedural Metropolis**:
   - 48+ detailed skyscrapers with procedural lit window maps and vibrant rooftop neon perimeters.
   - Asphalt avenues with yellow centerlines, curbs, and animated coastal ocean shoreline.
   - Ocean Drive coastal palm promenade with 70+ palm trees and sodium streetlights.

2. **High-Fidelity Vehicle Dynamics**:
   - **Veloce GTS Supercar**: Aerodynamic body, carbon fiber wing spoiler, glowing LED headlamps, ruby lightbar, independent wheel rotation, and drifting physics.
   - Ambient civilian traffic patrolling the city avenues.

3. **CPD Law Enforcement & Pursuit AI**:
   - 1 to 5 star wanted level system.
   - Black & white CPD interceptor cruisers with alternating red/blue strobe lights and dynamic point illumination.
   - Intercept pursuit AI, roadblock barricades, and **CONDOR-1 Tactical Helicopter** featuring a high-intensity volumetric searchlight directed at the player.

4. **Combat Arsenal**:
   - 5-tier arsenal: *Solis .45*, *Coral-9 SMG*, *CAR-80 Carbine*, *Enforcer 12G Shotgun*, and *Titan RPG*.
   - Ballistic raycasting, dynamic muzzle flash point lights, visual tracers, and explosive rocket physics.

5. **Cyberpunk HUD & Minimap**:
   - Real-time 2D radar minimap with player arrow, police blips, and mission markers.
   - Speedometer gauge, gear indicator, wanted stars, health/armor bars, hitmarkers, and objective toasts.

6. **Web Audio Sound Synthesis**:
   - 100% procedural Web Audio API audio synthesis: dynamic engine frequency ramps, gunfire bursts, dual-tone oscillating police sirens, sub-bass explosions, and mission stingers.

7. **8 Narrative Campaign Missions**:
   - *Neon Horizon*, *Veloce Heist*, *Ocean Drive Showdown*, *CPD Evade*, *Skyline Strikeforce*, *Coral Syndicate Bust*, *Harbor Infiltration*, and *Grand Finale: Solis Kingpin*.

## Controls

| Action | Key / Input |
|---|---|
| Move Forward / Throttle | `W` / `Up Arrow` |
| Move Backward / Brake / Reverse | `S` / `Down Arrow` |
| Steer Left / Strafe Left | `A` / `Left Arrow` |
| Steer Right / Strafe Right | `D` / `Right Arrow` |
| Handbrake / Drift | `Space` |
| Sprint | `Shift` |
| Enter / Exit Vehicle | `F` or `E` |
| Aim / Fire Weapon | `Left Mouse Click` |
| Weapon Selection | `1` - `5` |
| Reload | `R` |

## Cinematic Staging & Screenshots

Solis City supports deterministic scene staging via query parameters:
- `?shot=chase`: 4-star Ocean Blvd pursuit with drifting supercar, trailing CPD cruisers with red/blue strobes, roadblock, and CONDOR-1 searchlight.
- `?shot=combat`: Downtown Coral Bank firefight with aiming CAR-80, glowing muzzle flash, bullet tracers, and alert status.
- `?shot=street`: Sunset cruise on Ocean Drive with golden hour lighting, palm trees, skyscraper skyline, and luxury traffic.
- `?shot=title`: Solis City twilight panorama overlooking 44 skyscrapers and ocean bay.
